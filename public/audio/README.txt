Drop a seamless looping ambient track here named exactly:

    ambient-loop.mp3

The AmbientPlayer control (bottom-left of the site) loads /audio/ambient-loop.mp3
and loops it at a low volume (0.12). Until this file exists the toggle stays in
the "Sound Off" state and does nothing — no errors, it just waits for the track.

Tips for a good loop:
- 20–60s, fades trimmed so the end meets the start cleanly (no click/gap).
- Keep it textural/atmospheric (room tone, soft pad, vinyl noise) — it sits UNDER
  everything, so anything melodic/loud becomes intrusive fast.
- Export ~128 kbps MP3 to keep the download light.
